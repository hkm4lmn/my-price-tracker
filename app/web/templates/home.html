How it works

  1. Send the bot a link to a product page.
  2. It reads the price and checks again every six hours.
  3. You get a message when the price drops. Use /target to only hear about prices below a number you choose.

Which shops work

Shops that publish standard product information on their pages, which many Malaysian shops do for search engines. Machines and Senheng work, for example. Shopee doesn't: it blocks automated price checks, and this tracker doesn't try to get around that.
